Netflix is launching a TikTok-style vertical video feed this month and expanding its use of artificial intelligence for content recommendations and creation, according to announcements made during the company's first-quarter earnings call.

The streaming giant has been testing the short-form vertical video feature since 2025, designed to help users discover video podcasts alongside traditional shows and movies. This represents Netflix's continued pivot toward short-form content consumption.

Regarding AI improvements, co-CEO Gregory Peters stated: "We see tremendous room to make it better by leveraging newer technologies." The company plans to implement new recommendation systems that will improve personalization while enabling faster iteration across different content types.

Co-CEO Ted Sarandos emphasized that artificial intelligence will enhance the creative process itself. He noted that while great artists remain essential to great art, AI tools will help creators realize their visions more effectively.

Netflix recently acquired InterPositive, Ben Affleck's AI filmmaking company, which the company believes strengthens its generative AI capabilities. Sarandos reported that creators have already shown interest in these proprietary filmmaking tools.

The company also intends to leverage AI for its advertising suite, seeking to improve ad formats and customization for better returns.
