San Francisco-based Loop has secured $95 million in Series C funding to develop artificial intelligence systems that predict and prevent supply chain disruptions. The round was led by Valor Equity Partners and the Valor Atreides AI Fund, with additional backing from 8VC, Founders Fund, Index Ventures, and J.P. Morgan's Growth Equity Partners.

Co-founder and CTO Shaosu Liu explained the company's vision through a health analogy: "The end goal is someone teaching me about nutrition, someone teaching me about longevity," rather than just identifying problems after they occur.

Liu and CEO Matt McKinney, who previously worked together at Uber, plan to allocate significant capital toward engineering talent recruitment. The company transforms unstructured data—including PDFs, paper documents, and digital messages—into organized information to automate supply chain tasks and identify cost savings and risk areas.

The startup is expanding its capabilities by integrating with enterprise resource planning systems, transportation management platforms, and supply chain partners to enable predictive intelligence rather than just diagnostic analysis.
