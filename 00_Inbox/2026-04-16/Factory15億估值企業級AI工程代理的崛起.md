Factory, a startup developing AI agents for enterprise engineering teams, has secured $150 million in funding at a $1.5 billion valuation. The round was led by Khosla Ventures, with support from Sequoia Capital, Insight Partners, and Blackstone.

According to founder Matan Grinberg, the company's distinguishing feature is "its ability to switch between different foundation models, such as Anthropic's Claude or Chinese AI startup DeepSeek." However, competitors like Cursor offer similar flexibility across multiple AI models.

The three-year-old startup counts engineering teams at Morgan Stanley, Ernst & Young, and Palo Alto Networks among its customers.

Grinberg founded Factory in 2023 after dropping out of his PhD program at UC Berkeley, following encouragement from Sequoia partner Shaun Maguire. The two connected over shared academic interests in physics, with Maguire holding a PhD from Caltech in the same field Grinberg was studying.

This funding reflects investor confidence in AI-assisted coding, which remains the most widely adopted and profitable application of generative AI technology, despite competition from major players including Anthropic, Cursor, and Cognition.
