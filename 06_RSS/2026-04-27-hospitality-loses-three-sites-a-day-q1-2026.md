## 全文

# Hospitality Loses Three Sites a Day in Tough First Quarter of 2026

**Author:** NIQ/CGA intelligence

**Publication Date:** April 27, 2026

Britain's hospitality sector experienced significant challenges during the first quarter of 2026, with licensed premises declining at an accelerating rate. According to NIQ's Hospitality Market Monitor, the country lost an average of 3.4 outlets daily, resulting in a 0.3% overall decline from December 2025.

The report documented 98,609 licensed premises by March 2026, representing a loss of 305 establishments. This marks the second consecutive quarter-on-quarter decline in the sector.

## Primary Challenges

Multiple cost pressures contributed to the closures. Karl Chessell, Director of Hospitality Operators and Food at CGA by NIQ, noted that "soaring costs have taken a heavy toll on hospitality in the first quarter and forced hundreds of businesses to close."

The primary drivers included:
- Rising labor expenses
- Elevated energy costs
- Increased food and beverage prices
- Weakening consumer confidence
- Geopolitical tensions affecting energy markets

## Sector-Specific Performance

The casual dining segment suffered particularly, with a 0.9% decline over three months. Bar establishments also faced significant pressure due to reduced discretionary consumer spending.

The accommodation sector demonstrated greater resilience, showing year-on-year growth in licensed hotels and remaining only 4.7% below pre-COVID levels compared to a broader 14.3% sector decline.
