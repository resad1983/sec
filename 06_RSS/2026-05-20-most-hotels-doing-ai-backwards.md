# Most Hotels Are Doing AI Backwards

**Author:** Vassilis Syropoulos, CEO and Head of Product, Juyo Analytics

**Publication Date:** May 20, 2026

Hotels frequently adopt AI technology first, then search for problems it might address. This backward approach results in multiple pilot projects across different departments without clear metrics or measurable outcomes. Within six months, organizations often abandon these initiatives without achieving meaningful financial impact.

The fundamental issue stems from treating AI as a strategic priority rather than as a practical tool. According to the author, "Tools don't matter until you've named the job."

## The Five-Step Framework

**Step 1: Map Weekly Pain Points**

Identify recurring tasks consuming over two hours weekly, requiring data from multiple systems, or involving decisions that get postponed due to tedious processes. Examples include pricing reviews, group quote turnaround, month-end reporting, review responses, and forecast updates.

**Step 2: Ask Three Critical Questions**

- Is the bottleneck information or judgment?
- Does the task have clear inputs and outputs?
- Can you define what "good" looks like?

**Step 3: Select One Workflow**

Focus exclusively on the task with the highest time-saving potential and clearest quality metrics.

**Step 4: Run a 30-Day Measurable Trial**

Establish baseline metrics before implementation, define quality thresholds, and assign ownership to track hours saved and errors reduced.

**Step 5: Expand Gradually**

Only introduce additional workflows after demonstrating that the initial implementation generates positive returns.

## Implementation Philosophy

Successful organizations in 2026 will focus on eliminating weekly meetings and manual processes rather than accumulating numerous tools. The author emphasizes identifying friction points first, then applying appropriate technology solutions strategically.
