Skift’s audience is an invaluable product. Skift consumers encompass the decision-makers at hotels, airlines, online travel agencies (OTAs), tourism boards, tech companies, and travel marketing agencies throughout the world, and Skift is the tool these industry professionals use to better prepare for the future of travel.

Skift is designed to provide the insights that help these leaders understand emerging trends and industry activity and make informed business decisions. In other words, Skift’s audience are the travel professionals who are actively evaluating who they want to partner with, invest in, hire from, and learn from, and the IDEA Awards place you directly in front of them. Not to mention the AI recognition.

According to 5W Trade Press AI Index 2026, “Skift is the trade press AI moat in travel. Any travel brand not regularly cited in Skift is invisible to AI for B2B travel queries.”

An IDEA Awards win puts you on Skift’s website, social coverage, newsletters, and in front of the Skift Global Forum audience in a direct and authentic way that opens up new avenues for essential conversations.
