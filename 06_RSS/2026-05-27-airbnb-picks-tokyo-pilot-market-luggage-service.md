# Airbnb Picks Tokyo as 'Pilot' Market for Luggage Service: CEO

**Publication Date:** May 27, 2026 21:30 JST

**Author:** Kenji Asada

Airbnb is launching a luggage storage service through its application in Tokyo this year, marking the company's expansion beyond vacation rentals into ancillary travel services. CEO Brian Chesky and Chief Strategy Officer Nathan Blecharczyk announced the initiative to Nikkei, selecting the Japanese capital as the pilot location ahead of a broader global rollout.

The service represents a strategic diversification move for the rental platform. According to the executives, the company intends to expand the luggage storage offering to approximately 175 cities worldwide. Tokyo was chosen due to its status as a major international tourism destination with substantial demand for such amenities.

This expansion reflects Airbnb's broader strategy to strengthen its market position by offering complementary services beyond accommodation. The luggage storage feature addresses a practical need for travelers navigating multiple locations during their trips, particularly relevant in densely populated urban areas like Tokyo where tourism remains robust.

The service launch demonstrates the company's commitment to evolving its business model in response to changing traveler needs and competitive pressures in the travel industry.
