# Win over India's Travellers in 2026: New Insights and Strategies for Your Brand to Take Off

**Authors:** Sourav Nandi, Bishmay Rout

**Publication Date:** November 2025

This Think with Google article examines travel marketing strategies for India's consumer market, drawing on research conducted with Kantar to identify emerging travel trends and effective AI-powered advertising approaches.

## Key Consumer Insights

The research identifies two critical touchpoints influencing Indian travellers' purchasing decisions: YouTube and Google. According to the study, "68% of travellers use YouTube," with content ranging from activity guides to traveller vlogs. Short-form video is particularly influential—two in five travellers rely on YouTube Shorts for inspiration, making it the leading short-form platform for travel content in India.

Creators significantly impact travel planning, with "41% of surveyed travellers are highly reliant on influencers during trip planning." Additionally, nearly 44% of travellers spend more than a week planning their vacations, using multiple touchpoints for research.

## Marketing Strategy Recommendations

The article recommends a two-pronged approach for travel brands:

**Discovery Strategy:** Brands should use "engaging, multiformat video ads and AI-powered campaigns on YouTube" to enhance visibility. The MakeMyTrip case study demonstrates this approach, achieving a "16% business uplift" through YouTube Masthead, First Position in Shorts, and non-skippable in-stream ads.

**Conversion Optimization:** Brands should leverage three interconnected AI tools: Performance Max campaigns for cross-channel reach, AI Max for Search to access new queries, and Travel Feeds in Search Ads for dynamic pricing displays. OYO's implementation of Performance Max achieved "50% higher return on ad spend and 25% lower cost per acquisition."

## Traveller Archetypes

The research categorizes Indian travellers into four segments:

1. **Novice Travellers** (67% of respondents): Cost-conscious first-time travellers seeking value-for-money packages
2. **Globetrotters** (59%): International travel enthusiasts spending 3.2X more on international trips, seeking premium experiences
3. **Religious Pilgrims** (44%): Travelers seeking spiritual destinations and cultural experiences, researching across diverse language preferences
4. **Memory Makers** (39%): Experience-focused travellers planning around events and cultural moments, highly reliant on creator partnerships

The data suggests tailoring marketing messages and ad placement strategies to align with each segment's distinct preferences and research behaviors.
